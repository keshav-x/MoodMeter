How MoodMeter Classifies Feedback

MoodMeter evaluates customer feedback using Natural Language Processing (NLP). It computes sentiment polarity (-1.0 to +1.0), tags relevant department themes (e.g. Quality, App, Billing), and checks for critical safety hazards or defect triggers (e.g. fire, burn, overheat). Urgent feedback is immediately routed to designated teams for resolution.
